# Logos

These logos are the trademarks of their owners. folio uses them only to name the apps and services
it works with, next to their names (the formats it opens and writes, the AI providers its agent can
use, the other lsuite apps); it isn't endorsed by or affiliated with them. Their colours are kept.
Simple Icons artwork is CC0. Each file was rasterised to 128 × 128 px (resvg for SVG, Pillow for
PNG) and otherwise left as it was. Downloaded on 2026-10-07. `ui::logos::LOGOS` maps ids to files.
